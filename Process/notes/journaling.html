This was a lot. But I also know that this was me at my best. The real, true me starting to live, instead of just breathe beneath my skin. I don’t have to worry if this will happen again, if I work on myself. If I truly love myself, and If I trust and have faith on what I’m doing, this will continue to happen, much more frequent than now.

Im me. Im my vision, I’m my creativity. But at the same time, all of that lives outside of me. I have the key to all doors, but I have to unlock them anyways. If I sit, and dwell on thoughts with my key on my hand, I’ll never get anything done. Thats where intuition plays a crucial role. Intuition tells me the quantity of the doors, reveals new ones, and discourages old ones. Intuition tells me which doors are the ones that are more obviously important, but still lets me have faith on doors that I might not understand fully.

My vision is my key. My works are the doors. I must work every day to use my key. To develop my key so it’s essentially form-less, able to open every door in its own way. They wont leave me. Im the key, The vision is out there FOR ME. These doors I only see.

This is the start of the beginning. And doors are yet to be opened, but by work, faith and trust in myself, and in the highness.
